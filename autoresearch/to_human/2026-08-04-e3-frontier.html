Why this matters for the product

The displayed "you saved $3.73/run" is budget headroom, already disavowed in the codebase. The honest number now exists: a settled task costs ~$0.001 on the routed lane; the same tokens at frontier list price would cost several times that. E4 ships the per-run counterfactual delta — small, true, and defensible — plus decline-with-refund for the two classes where taking the money would be dishonest. That last part is the thesis: a marketplace that proves what it cannot do is the one whose savings number you can believe.

Method notes a reviewer would ask about

Grader is reference-anchored but same-family as workers (named caveat on all accept rates). Worker/grader ride distinct Azure purposes, so the terra A/B never touched the grader. Per-agent accept drifts ±6pp at single-sweep scale — all policy claims rest on pooled n. Near the pass threshold, mean accuracy inverts (mini "outscores" terra while both fail) — pass rate is the only trustworthy escalation signal. Probe-gating and auction-layer experiments stay deferred until the live roster exceeds 2 agents; claiming otherwise would outrun the data.
